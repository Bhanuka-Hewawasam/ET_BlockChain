Instructions to the students 
Emerging Technology for a Smarter Future – Innovate, Build, and Transform 
You are part of a technology innovation team appointed by a local government authority to 
develop innovative solutions that improve the quality of life of citizens through the effective 
use of emerging technologies. 
The authority has identified several challenges in the community, including inefficient waste 
management, traffic congestion, energy wastage, public safety, limited access to essential 
services, and inefficient use of public resources. 
Your team has been tasked with identifying one specific real-world problem and proposing an 
innovative, technology-driven solution. The proposed solution must integrate at least two 
emerging technologies, such as Artificial Intelligence (AI), Internet of Things (IoT), Cloud 
Computing, Blockchain, Robotics, Augmented Reality (AR), Virtual Reality (VR), or Big Data 
Analytics. 
You are expected to investigate the selected problem, analyze existing solutions, design a 
proposed system, and develop a working prototype or simulation that demonstrates the 
feasibility and potential impact of your solution. 
Your proposed solution should demonstrate how emerging technologies can be integrated to 
improve efficiency, enhance user experience, reduce costs, or create measurable social and 
environmental benefits.

Avoid Plagiarism 
Any artifacts submitted by students which are related to the given assignment are accepted with 
the strict understanding that it's their own property and If evidence of plagiarism is identified 
then the work submitted by those students will result in zero marks being awarded. 
Maximum allowed plagiarism percentage rate is 20%., AI tools usage should be 0% percentage. 
Evaluation Criteria 
• Individual contribution of each member should be reflected at the presentation. The 
lecture expects that everyone will highlight their own work. 
• There must be a slide in the presentation that concisely illustrates the contribution of 
each member.  
• The lecture will pick any individual based on their preference and ask questions related 
to the content presented after the presentation. 
• Each member of the team should have a broad understanding on the entire work, 
proposed solution, strategies followed and implementation regardless of the individual 
contribution.  
• Marks will be allocated at the time of evaluation in the following manner  
o Relevance of problem and solution    
o Innovation and creativity     
o Effective use of emerging technologies   
o Quality of prototype (functionality/UI)   
o Report clarity and completeness    
o Presentation
